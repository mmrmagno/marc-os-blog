A Jellyfin plugin that adds **Anime** and **Movies & TV** as channels. AniList and TMDB are the catalogs, the video comes from online sources through a relay inside the plugin, and nothing is downloaded.

More in the [blog post](/blog/streamfin-streaming-instead-of-storing-in-jellyfin).
